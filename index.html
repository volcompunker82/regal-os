Pricing ideas to consider

These published examples are from other companies and are not Regal prices or local recommendations. Review them as categories only; enter and confirm your own policy.

  • Stairs or steps, landings, halls, and oversized rooms
  • Fabric protector, odor treatment, or specialty spot treatment

  • Upper-floor equipment/setup where access changes the work
  • Upholstery by seat or piece; minimum service charge
